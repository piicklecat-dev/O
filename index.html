🦍 Monke Tag VR

Touch the ground, a wall or a tree and push or swing your arms to launch yourself. Hold grip to stick to a surface and climb, then let go to throw yourself. Right trigger fires your blaster and stuns a monkey for a few seconds.

Fourteen monkeys are playing tag. Red glowing = “it.” Don’t get caught. If you’re it, touch someone to pass it on. You get a 2-second head start after every tag.

VR tip: the virtual body and shoulders sit lower so your arms can reach the ground more naturally. Stand or crouch, whichever feels good.
